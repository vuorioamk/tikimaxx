Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026 · Personal-use app, version 4.0

This policy describes the current behavior of TikiMaxx, a locally installed iPhone app in active development. This public informational website does not connect to your Oura account or request your health records.

1. Health and nutrition information

After you authorize access, TikiMaxx can request daily health and activity summaries and workouts from Oura, and permitted measurements from Apple Health. These can include energy expenditure, workouts, steps, sleep, heart-rate metrics, weight, body composition and dietary nutrients, according to the permission and device support available.

You can also record food, body weight, goals, and optional coaching text. Some estimates, including meal photography and activity-adjusted calorie goals, are approximate.

2. How the information is used

TikiMaxx presents summaries and trends and estimates energy and nutrition targets. Oura is preferred for supported daily metrics; Apple Health can provide additional or fallback data. The app attempts to avoid duplicate counting, but independent sources may disagree or contain incomplete records.

3. Local storage and credentials

The nutrition diary, local goals, preferences, and saved coaching records are stored in the app's local data on the iPhone. The Oura authorization token and ChatGPT authorization data are stored in the device's iOS Keychain. Health measurements are requested for display and calculations; this version does not use an independent, developer-operated health-data cloud database. Exported diary backups are stored wherever you choose to save them.

4. Connections and sharing

  • Oura: Requests for Oura data go to Oura's API only after you authorize the connection.
  • Apple Health: TikiMaxx reads permitted categories. Optional nutrition and weight writeback requires a separate opt-in.
  • OpenAI / ChatGPT: A meal description, selected photo, or coaching context is sent only after you explicitly request an AI feature. The receiving provider's policies also apply.
  • GitHub Pages: This informational site is served through GitHub Pages, which may process technical information needed to serve and secure the pages.

The current app does not include a separate advertising network or third-party behavioral analytics SDK.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect Oura, change Apple Health permissions in iOS, choose whether to share diary records with Apple Health, export your records and clear local data. Clearing local data does not erase records already held by external services. You may need to revoke remote access separately.

6. Data limitations and security

Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain and service requests use secure network connections. The app is under active development, so bugs, stale data and device sync delays are possible. Keep backups of important records. Do not use TikiMaxx as the sole source of medical information.
