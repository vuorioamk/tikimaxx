HOW DATA WORKS

Your sources, shown clearly.

Oura is the preferred source for supported daily measures. Apple Health supplements with weight, body composition and detailed workouts. TikiMaxx aims to avoid counting overlapping measurements twice.

The app is locally installed and experimental. Actual measurements depend on device permissions and source availability; syncing may be delayed by devices, cloud processing or iOS.
